Which factory
do you want to run?

Three art directions for the andon belt, phone-portrait first. The belt runs vertically — issues drop from the inbox at the top, ride through the four seed stations, and exit at the bottom as score (merged PR) or compost (closed, no PR). All three are the same screen re-skinned, which is exactly how the real app will treat themes.

The candidates

Same HUD (score · week · transit), same station cards from andon.toml, same lamps: green humming, amber waiting, red cord pulled.

The cord-pull moment

The emotional core of the app, shown in the recommended direction: the belt physically stops, the floor warms toward red, the pulled station takes over the screen, and the two actions you can take are one thumb away.

Recommendation

Ship A (Shop Floor) as the base, and steal C's glow for the lamps only. Flat industrial keeps every station label readable at phone size and renders cheaply, which protects the 60fps-belt requirement in the epic. The one bold element is light: andon lamps get the neon treatment (soft bloom, real glow), so green/amber ambience is calm and a red cord-pull is the single loudest thing the screen can do — which is the whole Toyota point.

B (Night Shift '89) is the most charming and worth keeping in a drawer — it would make a great unlockable theme once theming is cheap, since all three candidates here are token-swaps over the same markup.
